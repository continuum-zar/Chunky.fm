Music deserves more than an algorithm.

chunky.fm is a live internet radio station where everyone listening hears the same second of the same song. One person chooses the records. There is no shuffle, no skipping and no algorithm. Sessions happen on announced evenings and end when they end — the station is deliberately off air in between.

The room chooses the theme, and the set is put together over the day it plays. When the broadcast begins, every browser tuned in aligns itself to the station’s own clock, so people in different countries are inside the same bar of the same record at the same instant.

Some songs don’t need better recommendations. They need better introductions. So the music arrives with context beside it — where a record came from, what was happening around it, what to listen for — and with a room of people hearing it at the same time.

You’re not listening beside people. You’re listening with them. There is a chat, a way to ask for something, and a microphone whoever is on the decks can talk over the music with. Listeners can put a hand up and be brought on air to say something themselves.

There is no account and nothing to sign up for: you pick a name when you arrive and it stays in your own browser. Listening is free. When the station is off air, this page says when the next session is.
